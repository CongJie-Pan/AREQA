### 🤖 AREQA
mRNA vaccines work by delivering a temporary "instruction manual" that teaches your cells how to recognize and fight a virus without ever exposing you to the actual live pathogen. 🛡️

Here is how the process works step by step:

### 1. The Role of the Spike Protein in Infection
To understand the vaccine, it helps to look at the virus first. Many viruses (like SARS-CoV-2) have **spike proteins** sticking out of their outer surface. Think of the spike protein as a **"key"** that fits into specific "locks" (receptors) on human cells, allowing the virus to enter and cause infection.

---

### 2. Delivering the Blueprint (mRNA)
Instead of injecting a weakened or killed virus, an mRNA vaccine delivers a tiny strand of genetic messenger code—the **mRNA**. This code contains instructions solely for making the harmless spike protein, not the rest of the virus.

---

### 3. Protein Production
Once inside your muscle cells, the cell's natural protein-making machinery (ribosomes) reads the mRNA instructions and temporarily builds harmless copies of this spike protein. The mRNA itself is fragile and breaks down quickly after delivering its message.

---

### 4. Immune System Training
Your cell displays these new spike proteins on its surface. Your immune system recognizes them as foreign invaders and mounts a response by:
* Producing specialized **antibodies** that can latch onto the spike protein.
* Creating **memory cells** that remember what the spike protein looks like.

---

### 5. Future Protection
If the real virus enters your body later, your immune system already recognizes the "key" (spike protein) and neutralizes the virus before it can unlock your cells and make you sick.
