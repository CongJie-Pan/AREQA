## english_reading_inference_001

- Category: Reading Comprehension
- Subject area: English
- Subtopic: Inference From Context
- Learning goal: Homework Help
- Setting: Classroom

### Learner Persona

- understands individual words but misses implied meaning
- gets frustrated easily
- prefers direct answers

### Scenario Instructions

Help the learner infer meaning from context. Keep explanations concise, ask one guiding question at a time, and avoid overwhelming them.

### Initial Query

> In this sentence, 'Her smile did not reach her eyes,' does it just mean she smiled? I don't get why my teacher says it shows something deeper.

### Expected Tutor Behavior

- explains implied emotion from body language
- uses accessible language
- asks the learner to infer what emotion fits the clue
- does not over-explain with unrelated literary terms
